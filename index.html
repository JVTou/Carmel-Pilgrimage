A Pilgrimage of Prayer and Penance

Each October, a company of pilgrims gathers at St. Aloysius Retreat House before setting out for Monterey, to begin the walk south to Carmel. What began with twenty-seven pilgrims under the banner “Mary, Vanquisher of Satan” has continued as a yearly tradition: from El Estero Park, through the streets of Monterey, and on along the coast until the bell towers of the old mission rise above the trees.

It is a day offered rain or shine — the Rosary prayed decade by decade underfoot, hymns carried on the sea wind, small acts of penance folded into every mile. Pilgrims arrive footsore and singing, to kneel where Fr. Serra himself once knelt, and to entrust the intentions of their families, their parishes, and the whole Church to the Mother of God.

Join us on this pilgrimage and offer the day for your own special intentions, or for the intentions of our faithful.

“We go up on foot, as pilgrims always have, because some graces are only granted to those willing to walk for them.”
— A priest of the Retreat House, before the 2023 departure

The Way South

8:00 AM — Meet in Los Gatos

Pilgrims gather at St. Aloysius Retreat House. Donuts, coffee, and juice are served in the lower courtyard.

9:30 AM — Depart for Monterey

Cars leave Los Gatos together for the coast.

11:00 AM — Arrive at El Estero Park

971 Pearl Street, Monterey. Restrooms available; please bring your own lunch. Fr. Asher and Fr. Caley lead the company in prayer before setting out.

12:00 PM — The pilgrimage begins

On foot from Dennis the Menace Park, the Rosary begins as the company walks on through the streets of Monterey and into Carmel.

5:00 PM — Arrival at the Basilica

The company arrives at Mission San Carlos Borroméo (Carmel Mission) for a final prayer at the tomb of Fr. Serra.

5:45 PM — Pizza

Pizza is provided at the mission to close a day fifteen miles in the making.
